## Hardware Work 
<img src="/posts/images/1ums.jpg">
Way back in like April, I mentioned I was working on a little 1U Mid Side processor and I'm happy to report I finally got my shit together and racked it up. It works like a dream, and as I had hoped, running bandpassed outputs from Mannequins Three Sisters into the mid and side inputs results in weird phasey outputs that are precisely what I (and maybe you?) are after. 

My current ambition is wrap up a desktop preamp for Eurorack purposes. I want a more flexible way of grabbing signals from a stereo pair of microphones and feeding a clean copy to a mixer and routing an amplified copy to my case. Obviously, I could get it done with modules in a little pod case, but I'm a sicko for designing power supplies, so here I am. It'll have big old bipolar 15V rails for headroom, and a super quiet phantom power rail. 

## Music Work
Another big thing I'm chugging away on lately is editing an album for my collaboration with my good friend Harlan Kelly in [Squirming](https://squirming.bandcamp.com/). We got together almost two years ago and recorded a mountain of material--almost 8 hours. I've been dragging my feet ever since to edit that down and turn it into *something* and get it out there. I'm about 2/3rds of the way through and I'm really excited. It bridges the gap between the free-associative noise piles Harlan and I have always made with each other and a more focused, narrative push. At least in my opinion that's what it does. You can judge freely.
